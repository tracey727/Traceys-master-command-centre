# ARCHIVE INTEGRITY AND LIMITATIONS

- No dates were invented.
- Library creation timestamps were retained where available.
- Conversation dates came from the recovered dated conversation summary.
- An idea date is not automatically a legal priority date.
- A generated draft is not a signed agreement.
- A ZIP named “final” is not automatically the current production version.
- Patent placeholders such as “[Exact date]” must be replaced only from official filing receipts.
- The human and animal patent families must remain separate.
- This package does not provide legal, patent, privacy, engineering or clinical approval.
